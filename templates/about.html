Our mission

Kisan Route exists to shorten the distance — in price and in trust — between the people who grow food and the people who move, sell, and buy it. We do that by putting mandi rates, shared logistics, and direct market access in one place.

🌾 For farmers & clusters

Compare mandi rates before you sell, check crop quality, calculate net profit after transport, and track your shipment from pickup to delivery.

🛒 For customers

Buy directly from nearby farms with transparent pricing and simple order tracking.

🚚 For drivers

Find nearby loads, get an optimized route, and manage deliveries the way you would on any modern logistics app.

🏬 For wholesalers

Discover farmer listings near your mandi, filter by crop, and negotiate directly.

About this build

This is a hackathon prototype: the backend, routing, and logic are real Python (Flask); OTP, payments, crop-quality grading, and route optimization use clearly-marked mock modules so real APIs can be dropped in later without changing the rest of the app.
